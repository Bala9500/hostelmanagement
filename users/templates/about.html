About Murugan Hostel

A comfortable home away from home

Introduction

Murugan Hostel was established on 15 October 2020, during the challenging period of the COVID-19 pandemic. It was founded by Balasekaran from Tirunelveli with a vision to provide comfortable accommodation at an affordable price. The main goal of Murugan Hostel is to create a safe, peaceful, and welcoming environment for every resident. Every year, many people move to Chennai from different parts of Tamil Nadu for employment and higher education. Finding comfortable accommodation at a reasonable price can be difficult for students and working professionals. Murugan Hostel aims to make this journey easier by providing quality rooms without placing a heavy financial burden on residents. We focus on maintaining a clean, secure, friendly, and comfortable living environment. Our hostel is designed to make residents feel at home even when they are living away from their families. We continuously work to improve our facilities and provide better services based on the needs of our residents. Murugan Hostel is more than just a place to stay — it is a comfortable home away from home.

Rules and Regulations

To maintain a safe, peaceful, clean, and comfortable environment for everyone, all residents of Murugan Hostel are expected to follow these rules and regulations.

1. Drinking, Smoking & Drugs

Drinking alcohol, smoking, and the possession or use of illegal drugs are strictly prohibited inside the hostel premises. Any resident found carrying or using prohibited substances may be asked to vacate the hostel immediately, and appropriate action may be taken.

2. Pets

Pets are allowed in the hostel, provided they do not cause inconvenience to other residents. While taking pets outside the room, residents must keep them properly supervised and under control, using a leash or other appropriate safety measures where necessary.

3. Cleanliness & Room Maintenance

Our housekeeping staff will clean the rooms once every three days. However, residents are also responsible for keeping their rooms clean, hygienic, and well maintained between scheduled cleaning services.

4. Fighting & Misconduct

Fighting, threatening behaviour, harassment, or any form of violence is strictly prohibited within the hostel premises. Serious misconduct involving hostel residents or staff may result in removal from the hostel and, where appropriate, legal action.

5. Food & Dining

Food facilities are available for residents at an additional charge. We aim to provide hygienic, healthy, and quality meals. Non-vegetarian meals will be provided three days a week, according to the hostel's food schedule.

6. Hostel Fees

Hostel fees may vary depending on the room type, food plan, and available facilities. For complete information regarding room rent, food charges, deposits, and other fees, residents can contact our customer care team or visit Murugan Hostel directly.

7. Trial Period & Refund

New residents are provided with a four-day trial period to experience the hostel facilities. If a resident decides not to continue during this period, eligible fees will be refunded after deducting the applicable accommodation, food, and other expenses incurred during those four days, subject to the hostel's refund policy.

Note: By staying at Murugan Hostel, residents agree to respect these rules and cooperate with the management and fellow residents. These guidelines are intended to make the hostel a safe, clean, friendly, and comfortable home for everyone.
